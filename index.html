03 · Location

Where is it?

Central Asia, with a small part in Europe, west of the Ural River.

  • 5Neighbours: Russia, China, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan
  • ≈~7,600 km border with Russia, one of the longest land borders on Earth
  • ~Caspian Sea coast in the west
04 · Nature & climate

Steppe, mountains, lakes

Sharply continental climate: from −40 °C in winter to +40 °C in summer.

Steppe

Endless grasslands cover most of the country

Mountains

Tian Shan and Altai. Khan Tengri ≈ 7,000 m

Lakes

Balkhash is half fresh, half salty. Also Kolsai, Kaindy, Big Almaty Lake

Canyons

Charyn Canyon, about 150 km of red rock

05 · History

From nomads to independence

Independence Day is celebrated every year on 16 December.

  1. Ancient times
    Saka nomads; cities on the Great Silk Road (Otrar, Turkistan)
  2. 1465
    The Kazakh Khanate is founded
  3. 18th–20th c.
    Russian Empire, then the Soviet Union
  4. 16.12.1991
    Independence of the Republic of Kazakhstan
  5. 1997
    The capital moves from Almaty to Astana
06 · Government

Presidential republic

President: Kassym-Jomart Tokayev. A new constitution was approved in 2026, with a one-chamber parliament, the Kurultai.

Capital: AstanaConstitution 2026Anthem: «Menin Qazaqstanym»

Flag

Sky blue for peace and the open sky; golden sun with 32 rays, a steppe eagle and a national ornament

Emblem

A shanyrak (the top of a yurt, a symbol of home) on a sky-blue background, two winged horses (tulpars), a star and the word QAZAQSTAN

07 · Economy

Rich in resources

The largest economy in Central Asia, built on energy and metals.

Oil

Tengiz, Kashagan and Karachaganak fields

Gas

Big reserves, exported to China and Europe

#1

Uranium

World's largest producer, about 40% of global supply

Baikonur

First and largest spaceport. Gagarin launched here in 1961

08 · Culture

Nomadic traditions

Hospitality is a core value: a guest is always welcome at the table.

Yurt

Portable felt house of the nomads

Food

Beshbarmak, baursak and kumys

Music

The dombra, a two-string instrument

Nauryz

Spring New Year, 22 March
